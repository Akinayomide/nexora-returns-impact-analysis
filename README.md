## Project Overview
This project analyses product returns at Nexora Stores, focusing on return trends, affected products, customer activity, and the financial impact on the business.

Using Excel for initial data exploration and Power BI for visualisation, I developed an interactive dashboard to identify patterns in product returns, highlight areas of concern, and support data-driven business decisions.

## Business Problem 
Nexora Stores experienced a significant increase in product returns, raising concerns about revenue loss, profitability, and customer satisfaction.
Management needed to understand where the increase was coming from, which products and markets were most affected, and the financial impact of these returns.

## Project Objectives
The objective of this project was to analyse product return patterns and develop an interactive Power BI dashboard to support business decisions.
Specifically, the analysis aimed to:
- Track return trends and identify significant changes over time.
- Identify products, categories, countries, and customers contributing to returns.
- Measure return rates and quantify the financial impact on revenue and profitability.
- Highlight key findings and recommend areas for management attention.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Power Query
- PowerBI

## Key Findings
- Q4 2025 spike: 442 units were returned, compared with 68 in Q4 2024.
- Software concentration: Software accounted for 84.8% of Q4 2025 returned units.
- Geographic hotspots: India and France together accounted for approximately 67% of Q4 2025 returned units.
- Financial impact: Q4 2025 returned sales value reached $146.8K, with an estimated $78.8K gross-profit impact.

## Recommendations
- Investigate the sharp rise in Software returns, particularly Insight Basic in France and Connect Business / Secure Endpoint in India.
- Review customer accounts with high returned quantities.
- Monitor return rates in subsequent periods to determine whether the spike persists.
